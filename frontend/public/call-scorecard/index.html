How to use the call scorecard

It's your cheat sheet during a call. The left side is where you take notes. The right side is only for you: it suggests the package, questions to ask, and proof to share. The prospect never sees it.

Before the call

  1. Open the "Open pre-filled scorecard" button in the morning pre-call brief email. No email? Click Paste booking info and paste the calendar invite or their form answers.
  2. Read the pink Before the call box. Their form answers are what they actually told us.
  3. The research under it is automatic and can be wrong (wrong person, wrong company). Click × on anything that doesn't fit, or Undo auto-fill to start clean.

During the call

  1. Click Start call if you want a timer. It's just a clock.
  2. When they say something, click the matching button under What they said. Click again to undo.
  3. Jot what they say in Call notes, in any order. Skip anything that doesn't come up. Each topic has a suggested question tucked under it if you want one.
  4. Score the business 1–5 in Readiness scorecard (1 = not set up, 5 = running great).
  5. Watch the right side: the recommended package and price, Watch for warnings, Ideas for what to ask, and a case study you can mention. Case studies also pop up when you click something.
  6. Tough question? Press / and type a word ("guarantee", "too expensive", "podcast"). Answers come from our playbook.

After the call

  • Send to Nutshell: creates the company, contact, and lead (assigned to you) with your call notes. Pick your name at the top once. Sending again after Call 2 just adds the new notes to the same lead. Nutshell is slow, so give it a minute.
  • Copy CRM note: the same notes as text, if you need them anywhere else.
  • Copy proposal brief: paste into Claude to write the proposal.
  • Client one-pager: prints their readiness score to send them.

Good to know

  • It saves automatically, but only on this computer. New call clears it; Export JSON keeps a copy.
  • Use Call 1 · Discovery for first calls and Call 2 · Pitch for the pitch (it adds the money math and closing lines).
  • Only name one package and its "Lead with" price. The lower prices are only for a real price objection.
