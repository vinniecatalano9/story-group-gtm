How to use the call scorecard

Your notes go on the left. The right side is only for you: what to ask next, warnings, the package to recommend, and a client story to tell. The prospect never sees it.

Before the call

  1. Click "Open pre-filled scorecard" in the morning pre-call email. No email? Click Paste booking info and paste the calendar invite or their form answers.
  2. Read the pink Before the call box. Their form answers are what they told us. The research under it is automatic and can be wrong, so click × on anything that doesn't fit.

During the call

  1. Work down the left side, 1 to 6. Each section has a question you can try. A number turns green when that section is done.
  2. Click a button when you hear something (click again to undo). Score each business area 1 to 5 as it comes up. The line under each area tells you what you're rating.
  3. Glance right: Ask next tells you what to ask, Watch for flags problems, and the package card shows what to recommend and the price to say first.
  4. Tough question? Press / and type it, like "can you guarantee Forbes?" Press Enter for a short answer you can say. It only uses our playbook, so double-check anything that sounds off.
  5. Want a timer? Click Start call on the right. It's just a clock.

After the call (section 7)

  1. Send to Nutshell saves the lead with your notes. Pick your name next to it once. Sending again after Call 2 adds the new notes to the same lead. Nutshell is slow, so give it a minute.
  2. Client one-pager prints their readiness score to send them.
  3. Copy proposal brief and paste it into Claude to write the proposal.

Good to know

  • It saves on this computer only. Reset clears everything for a new call, and you get 10 seconds to undo.
  • Use Call 1 · Discovery for first calls and Call 2 · Pitch for the pitch. Call 2 adds the money math and closing lines.
  • Say one package and its first price. The lower prices are only for a real price objection.

Paste booking info

Paste the calendar invite, the booking-form email, or any form answers. Name, company, and their answers get pulled in; nothing leaves this browser.
